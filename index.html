Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about recording tasks, equipment, and payments.

Any smartphone running Android or iOS with a working rear camera capable of recording standard 1080p (Full HD) video at 30 frames per second is eligible. Popular models include Samsung Galaxy, Redmi, Realme, OnePlus, Vivo, Oppo, and iPhones.

You can use standard, inexpensive elastic phone head-strap mounts available on Amazon or Flipkart (costing ~₹250-₹350). When you sign up and pass our brief camera test, Plus AI can also reimburse the mount or send an official starter kit directly to your address!

Payments are calculated at ₹350 per verified recording hour. Payouts are processed weekly directly to your Indian bank account via IMPS/NEFT or to your preferred UPI ID (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).

Your privacy is strictly safeguarded. Our AI ingest system automatically detects and blurs bystander faces, car license plates, and sensitive documents. We never sell personal contact data to third parties.

You have 100% flexibility! You can record for as little as 30 minutes on weekends or up to 20+ hours a week depending on task availability in your dashboard.
